Start a club

Have an idea? Lead a club.

Clubs are a chance to get together with others who share your passions — books, art, geography, or anything in between. If you're in grade 10, 11 or 12, here's how to start one.

The procedure

  1. Come up with an idea. Clubs must be led by a student in grade 10, 11 or 12, and the theme must be school-appropriate. Proposals are reviewed before being added to the club list.
  2. Read the notes on this page and make sure you understand them.
  3. Check the club directory to make sure your idea hasn't been taken already.
  4. Fill out the club proposal form (sign in to your schoolathome account first). Apply as soon as possible so nobody else takes your idea!
  5. We'll be in touch. Once we receive your proposal, we'll contact you to coordinate your club's publication.

Good to know

💬

Online communication

Every club needs some kind of online communication — we recommend Google Chat through schoolathome accounts. Meetings can be online or in person, but online is recommended: in past years it's been harder to grow in-person clubs.

🛡️

Chat guidelines

Set chat guidelines right away. Chats must stay school-appropriate, and you may want to keep them on-topic too. The Student Clubs manager is included in every club chat space.

✅

Parental permission

Creating a club assumes parental permission.

🎟️

100% free

Starting a club costs nothing. No funding is provided by TLA.

🤝

Lead solo or with a friend

Clubs can be managed individually or co-led with a friend.

🎓

TLA students only

Clubs are limited to current TLA students. Want an alumnus to join for a special event? Get prior approval from Mr. Edmund Buskell.

🔁

Idea already taken?

We'll let you know. You're welcome to join the existing club, or co-manage it if the original leader(s) agree — another reason to submit early!

Questions?
